# 6. The window bound (`VWin`)

`VWin` is the archive's own statement with no published analogue: a bound, by exp(x^ε/2), on a log-weighted sum over all primes ℓ of the lengths of Ш¹(G_{E,S}, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e')) for a family of large abelian CM fields E and all twists e' below x^{401}. The reports find that it is proved by splitting Ш¹ into three pieces handled by three classical-looking methods, of which the bound on the "plus" part, a nonzero-integer annihilator built from U(3) congruences and a modularity input, is the least classical.

Status markers: [statement read], [proof outline read], [proof read in full], [inferred from names], [unread]. `AV/` abbreviates `Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/AutomorphicVarieties/`; `CB/` abbreviates `Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/`.

## Terms used on this page

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e) | the discrete module ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ with Galois acting through the (−e)-th power of the cyclotomic character |
| Ш¹(G_{E,S}, M) | classes in continuous H¹ of the Galois group of the maximal extension of E unramified outside S that restrict to zero at every place of S |
| length | ℤ_ℓ-module length, i.e. log_ℓ of the order for a finite ℓ-group |
| plus / minus part | eigenspaces of complex conjugation c; for twist e the minus part (eigenvalue (−1)^{e+1}) is the side tied to Bernoulli numbers / L-values, the plus part is the "Vandiver" side |
| Euler system | a compatible family of cohomology classes (Kolyvagin, Rubin, Kato) used to bound Selmer groups from above |
| ℤ_ℓ-tower, μ-invariant | the cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-extension of a field; Iwasawa's μ = 0 (Ferrero–Washington for abelian fields) controls class-group growth |
| Bernoulli number B_{k,ψ} | generalized Bernoulli number; essentially L(1 − k, ψ) |

## The statement

`UpperTheory.Top.VWin := ∀ ψ, VWinAt ψ 401`, with `VWinAt` and `VVle` in `CB/ExistsPageN.lean`. [statement read, to depth 2] (statements-review.md row 19; vacuity-check.md Q1–Q2)

In plain terms: for every primitive Dirichlet character ψ, every ε ∈ (0, 1/52) and every choice of "page" e(x) with 2^{e(x)} ≈ x^{2+8ε}, there are J ≥ 1 and X₀ such that for X ≥ X₀ and every window pair (q, d) at scale X^{1/J}, there is Y ∈ [X^{1/J}, X^{1/2}] such that for every x ∈ [Y, Y²]: for every number field E that is the fixed field inside ℚ(ζ_N) of

  {a : a ≡ ±1 mod 2^{e+2}, ψ(a) = 1, (a/q) = 1, (a/d) = 1},

and every finite set S of primes,

```
Σ_{ℓ ∈ S} log ℓ · Σ_{e'=1}^{⌊x^401⌋−1} length_{ℤ_ℓ} Ш¹(G_{E,S_ℓ}, ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e'))  ≤  sqrt(exp(x^ε)),
```

with each Ш¹ finite. S_ℓ is the set of places above ℓ. The window pair conditions are those of `WinPair` (page [03](03-analytic-endgame.md)). (statements-review.md row 19)

`VVle` and the Ш¹ in Lean (vacuity-check.md Q2) [statement read]:

```lean
def Sha (D : LData F Ω l) (e : ℤ) := Sha1 (qzTw D e) (locFam D.S D.A) (continuous_locFam D.S D.A)
def shaLen (D : LData F Ω l) (e : ℤ) : ℕ∞ := Module.length ℤ_[l] (Sha D e)
```

`VVle E t B` quantifies over all `ld : ∀ p, LData E Ω p`, asserts `shaLen (ld p) e ≠ ⊤` for 0 < e < t, and asserts Σ_{ℓ∈S} log ℓ · Σ_{e=1}^{t−1} (shaLen).toNat ≤ B.

**Verdict on the statement:** undetermined, since it has no published analogue. It has many magic constants (401, ε < 1/52, 2 + 8ε, ε/5, the residues mod 8 and 16). The reviewer's impression is that, via Poitou–Tate, these lengths are ℓ-parts of étale K-groups / H²(ℤ_ℓ(1+e)), so `VWin` is a quantitative bound of "Bernoulli-number size" over a huge family of abelian fields, and that this is the real content of the argument. (statements-review.md row 19)

### It is not vacuous

- **Window pairs exist.** `QH.pairEx` proves them, and the main path applies `VWin` to such a pair (vacuity-check.md Q1; page [03](03-analytic-endgame.md)).
- **`LData` is inhabited** (`ldataExists`, `Formalism/GaloisCohomology/GaloisCohomologyProofs/LdataNonempty.lean:48`) and essentially canonical: S is the set of places above ℓ, the cyclotomic character is forced, only the choice of places varies. [statement read] (vacuity-check.md Q2)
- **`shaLen` is a genuine Ш¹**: intersection of kernels of restriction to decomposition groups at places in S, with H¹ = continuous cocycles modulo coboundaries and G_S the quotient by the closed normal subgroup generated by inertia outside S. That the topology on `Ω ≃ₐ[F] Ω` is Mathlib's Krull topology is the reviewer's recollection of Mathlib, not checked. Archimedean places are omitted, which matters only for ℓ = 2 and real places; the fields E here are totally imaginary. [statement read] (vacuity-check.md Q2)

## How it is used

`VWin` is applied in `winPairs` (page [03](03-analytic-endgame.md)) and enters `core_gm`'s hypotheses through `HypV → divinN_of_hypV → DIVinN`, where it bounds the Ш¹ terms in the Ribet-style bound on each lift's congruence module (page [04](04-chain-and-one-half.md)). There only √b is needed against b, so the bound is used with room to spare. [proof read in full] (frontier-b-links.md Q1)

## The three-way split

`vvle_of_parts` (`LengthEqAddEigen.lean`, `PrimeSetBoundDataOfPrimitiveChar.lean`) splits Ш into ℓ = 2, the minus part for odd ℓ, and the plus part for odd ℓ, and gives VVle(E, t, B₂·log 2 + B_m + B_p). [statement read] (frontier-d-window.md Q1)

| Piece | Statement | Bound | Method | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| minus part, odd ℓ | `ThmBcFree` | `minusBound` = 3·n·t²·log(tN) + C·Σ_{ℓ \| o, ℓ odd} ℓ·n²·t²·log(tℓN)², polynomial | Kolyvagin–Rubin Euler system of Gauss/Jacobi sums (`PsiBound`), plus Stickelberger for ℓ \| [E:ℚ] | [proof outline read] |
| plus part, odd ℓ | `ThmHV` | (cond(E)·t)^C + C·exp(x^{4ε/5}) | counts I, II, III, V, Z: Ribet-style congruences on U(3) / GSp4, nonzero-integer annihilator | [proof outline read]; key inputs [unread] |
| ℓ = 2 and bad ℓ | `ThmBab` | exp(x^{4ε/5}) on some window [Y, Y²] | Iwasawa theory in the cyclotomic ℤ_ℓ-tower, μ = 0, pigeonhole over scales, `LemmaK` | [proof outline read] |

The target exp(x^ε/2) is far larger than all of these. The polynomial bounds are polynomial in the conductor N, which contains q·d with log(qd) ≤ x^{ε/4}. (frontier-d-window.md overview, Q3)

### Minus part: Euler systems

`PsiBound` (`AV/OddResidueBounds/ZeroMemUnrHom.lean:97`) [statement read]: for E abelian CM, ℓ odd, ℓ ∤ [E:ℚ], and ψ odd relative to e, the length of the ψ-part of Ш¹(ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ(−e)) is at most v(den·B_{e+1,ψ}). This is the upper-bound half of the cyclotomic main conjecture with Tate twists (classically Mazur–Wiles, or Kolyvagin–Rubin). It is proved as `ML.iv : PoitouTateDuality → UnramifiedOrthogonality → PsiBound` from a Poitou–Tate reciprocity law `Rec` and Kolyvagin derivative classes (`KolStep`, `KolHom`, `KolUnr`) of Gauss/Jacobi-sum units in `AV/PrimitiveFrobeniusFixed/` (7,350 lines). [proof outline read]; Kolyvagin internals [inferred from names]. The main conjecture is not stated as such. (frontier-d-window.md Q2)

Summing over ℓ: Σ_ℓ log ℓ · length ≤ Σ_ψ log|B_{e+1,ψ}·den|, polynomial in N and e. (frontier-d-window.md Q3)

### Plus part: the integer annihilator

The counts split by whether ℓ − 1 divides 2e' and whether ℓ lies in a fixed bad set `Bchi ψ` (`{3}` ∪ odd prime factors of ord(ψ)·ψ.N·p_K·h(ℚ(√−p_K))·p_1). Regular versus irregular primes play no role. [statement read] (frontier-d-window.md Q1)

| Count | Covers | Bound |
|---|---|---|
| `CountI` | ℓ ∉ Bchi and (ℓ−1) ∤ 2e' (generic) | over any finite set of such ℓ: Σ_ℓ log ℓ Σ_ψ Vn ≤ (N·t)^C |
| `CountZ` | ℓ ∉ Bchi, (ℓ−1) \| 2e', ψ = 1 | actually 0 (`PropZ`) |
| `CountII` | e' ≥ 2, chiP of even order, and (ℓ ∈ Bchi or (ℓ−1) \| 2e') | per ψ: (ℓ·N·e')^C, via GSp4 |
| `CountIII` | ℓ ∉ Bchi, (ℓ−1) \| 2e', ord ψ odd, ψ ≠ 1 | per ψ: (ℓ·N·e')^C, via GSp4 |
| `CountV` | ℓ ∈ Bchi, e' = 1, small R1 | per ψ: (ℓ·N·e')^C, via U(3) over ℚ(i) |
| `CountVs`, `CountIV` | remaining bad cases | proved internally (LemmaK and duality; no hypotheses) |

**The key statement**, `PropGK` (`AV/UnitaryThreeGroups/KatoUse.lean`) [statement read]:

```lean
def PropGK := ∀ K, ∃ c₅ A, ∀ e N H p, 1 ≤ e → p.Prime → ¬ p ∣ 30·N·disc K →
  ∃ NN : ℤ, NN ≠ 0 ∧ log|NN| ≤ c₅·φ([(ℤ/N)ˣ:H])·(N(e+1))^A·log p ∧
    ∀ l ≠ p, ∀ c, (∃ ψ ∈ orb c H, GD …) → (∀ ψ ∈ orb, Vlen ≠ ⊤) ∧ Σ_{ψ ∈ orb c H} Vn ≤ 2·v_l(NN)
```

With Σ_ℓ log ℓ·v_ℓ(NN) ≤ log|NN| (`corG_sum_log`), this bounds the sum over **all** ℓ at once. NN is a product of tr ρ(Frob_p) − (Ψ(Frob_p) + 1 + Ψ(Frob_p)^{−1}) over cohomological U(3) Galois representations ρ of bounded level and weight over K = ℚ(√−p_K). [proof outline read] (frontier-d-window.md Q2)

**Mechanism** (`OneCharU`, `FOneCharU.lean`) [proof outline read]: a nonzero class in the plus part gives a non-split residually Eisenstein deformation of ψ ⊕ 1 ⊕ ψ^{−1}. A graded deformation ring is built; every "type I" point is automorphic (`AdmI`, the modularity / R = T input), every "type II" point is an extension of a modular GL₂ representation. The pure commutative-algebra lemmas `LemmaM` / `LemmaM'` (`AV/CommutativeAlgebra/OrbitVanishingLocal.lean:111,120`, [statement read]) bound the Selmer length by 2·Σ over points of v(trace difference). So it is "Selmer ≤ congruence module", computed at one Frobenius, not Bloch–Kato via L-values and not a main conjecture. Kato's Euler system (`KatoQuadraticCharacters/`, 23.4k lines) is formalized and serves as unobstructedness input (`KatoTwoW`), [inferred from names] for the role. (frontier-d-window.md Q2)

**The modularity input** (frontier-g-deep-inputs.md §II). [statement read]; top-level proofs [proof outline read]
- `AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficientsAlt` (`DTC/AutomorphyUnramifiedCoefficients.lean:23`, with `DTC/` = `AV/ModularityArgument/ThresholdArguments/DenseThresholdCases/`) asserts that every type-I point of the deformation ring is automorphic and every type-II point has a modular 2-dimensional piece. It reduces to `AutEnd3Alt`: **every ordinary, strongly irreducible characteristic-zero lift of the Eisenstein residual representation ψ ⊕ 1 ⊕ ψ^{−1} to the C-group of U(3) is automorphic**, i.e. a residually reducible ordinary modularity-lifting theorem for U(3) / GL₃ over a CM field. The ring is Galois-theoretic, so this is not "R defined from automorphic data", and `Row0b` is not run in reverse.
- It is derived from four pieces: Hida theory for unitary groups (`HidaUAlt`; the theorem inhabiting it was not traced, so where it is proved is undetermined); **`PfromAut`** (`AV/PatchingModules/TaylorWilesObstructionBound.lean:150`), a Taylor–Wiles patching theorem for universal ordinary determinant (pseudo-deformation) rings: every irreducible component through a nice modular point is modular, and modularity propagates along chains of components linked through "thick" or "stable" primes; automorphic seed points from adjoint lifts of GL₂ Hida forms congruent to Eisenstein series; and a linking argument (`twoRuns_alt`, `seedPtOf_alt`, [inferred from names]) plus descent.
- This is the Skinner–Wiles / Thorne / Allen–Newton–Thorne strategy. `PfromAut` is stated **for all n and arbitrary residual pseudo-representations** over CM fields. In the published literature such statements need extra hypotheses (shape of ρ̄, "nice" conditions, n = 2 in Allen–Newton–Thorne). The archive's `Nice` / `thick` / `stable` predicates may encode them; the reviewer did not verify that, and calls it "the single place in (II) most worth an expert's time". `AV/PatchingModules/` has 441 files and 50,353 lines.
- `LemmaM` / `LemmaM'` [statement read]: a ℤ-graded O-algebra (weights under conjugation by diag(t, 1, t⁻¹)) with a reducible base point x; under smoothness, finiteness, one-sidedness, unobstructedness (M4) and a non-vanishing element a (M5), the degree −1 cotangent length is ≤ 2·Σ_{ξ∈T} v(ξ(a)) over a finite set T of "two-signed" (type I and II) points; and `LemmaM'` embeds the Selmer group into that cotangent space. The reviewer places it closest to Bellaïche–Chenevier (reducibility ideal) combined with a Wiles / Lenstra-type numerical inequality, with Mazur's Eisenstein-ideal measure read at one Frobenius. Its top-level proofs are short ([proof outline read]; `GradedModulesLength/` has 50 files, 6,625 lines) and "much less of a risk" than the patching step.

`Row0b` enters here three times: GL₂ local–global compatibility in Kato, Galois representations of U(3) forms, and a modularity lemma (frontier-d-window.md Q2).

There is no declaration named Vandiver, Lichtenbaum or Leopoldt. `PropGK` is the functional replacement for a "weakened Vandiver": it bounds the size of Vandiver-type eigenspaces by an integer; it does not prove they vanish. The only exact vanishing statement is `PropZ` (ψ = 1, e even, (ℓ−1) | 2e). (frontier-d-window.md Q3)

### ℓ = 2 and bad primes: Iwasawa theory and pigeonhole

`ThmBab`, from `thmBab_of_lemmaK … FW.ferreroWashington hK` [proof outline read] (frontier-d-window.md Q1, Q4):
- **μ = 0**: an in-archive proof of Ferrero–Washington via Sinnott's method (`AV/GaloisLibrary/FerreroWashingtonIndex/`, 62 files, 4,672 lines; `FW.ferreroWashington`, `FerreroWashington.lean:104`). Statement [statement read] (frontier-g-deep-inputs.md §III.1):

  ```lean
  ∀ l [Prime] K [NumberField] [IsAbelianGalois ℚ K], (∃ ζ : K, IsPrimitiveRoot ζ (if l = 2 then 4 else l)) →
    ∃ r, ∀ n K' [IsCyclotomicExtension {l^n} K K'], #(Cl(K') / Cl(K')^l) ≤ l^r
  ```

  i.e. bounded l-rank of the class group up the cyclotomic tower, **including l = 2**. Proof skeleton [proof outline read]: Stickelberger annihilation, a "Stickelberger ≢ 0 mod l" statement from a Fourier-dual form of Sinnott's independence lemma (`sinnott`, `sinLem`, about 365 lines, [proof read in full] for `sinnott`), genus theory for a uniform generator bound, and Leopoldt reflection for the plus part. No main conjecture, no class number formula and no normal-number input. The reviewer calls it "the full classical theorem … in its rank-bound form" following Sinnott's route faithfully at the level of skeleton and statements; `memZ`, `reps` and the Stickelberger directory are [unread]. (frontier-g-deep-inputs.md §III)
- **Uniform generator bound**: `towerTwo` (`AV/QuadraticExtensions/IsAlgClosureRat.lean:87`) gives R = 4(g + 6·deg M), uniform in q and d. **This is where q ≡ 3 mod 8 and d ≡ 7 mod 16 enter**: they fix the 2-adic splitting and ramification so each quadratic step adds bounded rank ([statement read]; `quadStep` [unread]).
- **Root avoidance**: shaInv(L_m, e') ≤ R'·(max(m, j₀) + c + B), where B is the ℓ-adic closeness of e' to one of ≤ R roots of the Iwasawa characteristic polynomial; at most one e' per root is exceptional.
- **Pigeonhole** over J = 2^{R+2}(2·C_K·Ct/ε)^R scales [Y, Y²] finds a window free of large exceptional twists. Uniform R makes J independent of the window. The reviewer compares this to a pigeonhole on F_{q^n}/F_q, with twists as field extensions and Iwasawa roots as Frobenius eigenvalues.
- **`LemmaK`** (`GeneratorCountTuples/LemmaK.lean`) [statement read] bounds the few small exceptional twists:

```lean
def LemmaK (CK : ℝ) : Prop := ∀ l L Ω (D : LData L Ω l), IsTotallyComplex L → HasMuQ l L → ∀ e ≥ 1,
  Finite (H2 (zlTw D (e+1))) ∧ Real.log (Nat.card (H2 (zlTw D (e+1)))) ≤ exp((2·e·[L:ℚ])^CK · log(2·|disc L|))
```

  This is the shape of Soulé's torsion bounds for K_{2e}(O_L), proved via group homology of GL_n(O_L) in `LiteratureResults/KTheoryStability/` (286 files, 34,368 lines). [statement read]; proofs [unread]
